### Title IX / Office of Equal Opportunity — Designated Reporter Statement

The George Washington University (GW) and its faculty are committed to maintaining a positive environment for study and work, in which individuals are judged solely on relevant factors, such as skill and performance, and can pursue their activities in an atmosphere that is free from discrimination, harassment, and violence. Individuals who wish to report that they have experienced discrimination or harassment based on a protected characteristic, including sexual assault, dating and domestic violence, and stalking may do so using a [reporting form](https://equalopportunity.gwu.edu/report-now) contained on the [Office of Equal Opportunity](https://equalopportunity.gwu.edu/)'s website. Please note that all GW Community Members, including faculty, who become aware of discrimination and harassment based on a protected characteristic are required to report this information to the OEO. If OEO receives information about an incident, outreach will be conducted to the impacted individual(s) that contains information about resources, rights, and procedural options. Community members are not required to respond to OEO outreach. If you, or another student you know, wishes to speak to a confidential resource who does not have this reporting responsibility, please contact Counseling and Psychological Services through the Student Health Center 24/7 at 202-994-5300, or the Office of Advocacy and Support for Title IX matters at 202-994-0443 or oas@gwu.edu.

### Mental Health Resources

Counseling and Psychological Services (CAPS), part of the [GW Student Health Center](https://healthcenter.gwu.edu/counseling-and-psychological-services), provides free, confidential counseling and psychological services to GW students. If you are in crisis, or concerned about a GW student in crisis, call 24 hours a day, seven days a week at **(202) 994-5300** (option 3) to speak with a counselor.

### Religious Observance

Students must notify faculty during the first week of the semester — as early as possible, but no later than **three weeks prior to the absence** — of their intention to be absent from class on their day(s) of religious observance. (If the observance falls within the first three weeks of the semester, students must inform faculty within the first week of the semester.) Faculty who intend to observe a religious holiday must arrange at the beginning of the semester to reschedule missed classes or to make other provisions for their course-related activities. Student members of all religious groups are entitled to the same courtesies and accommodations. Please see the [GW Religious Observance Policy](https://provost.gwu.edu/religious-observance-policy) for the full policy, final-exam rescheduling provisions, and the current [Religious Observance Calendar](https://students.gwu.edu/religious-observance-calendar-2025-2026).
