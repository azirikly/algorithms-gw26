## Getting Help

TAs and lab instructors are there to help you — if you feel stuck at any point during lab, please reach out and ask. Struggling is a normal part of the process; asking for help early is the fastest way through it.

## Attendance

**Attendance is mandatory** and will be taken by the TAs each session. If you are unable to attend, please notify us **as soon as possible**, along with the reason for your absence.

## Assessments

- **Lab quizzes:** There will be **6 lab quizzes** over the course of the semester, given during lab sessions. Your **lowest lab quiz score will be dropped**. Lab quizzes are closed-book and closed-technology — see the Syllabus for full details.
- **Homework assignments:** There will be **3 homework assignments** during the semester, assigned and submitted per the instructions provided with each assignment.
