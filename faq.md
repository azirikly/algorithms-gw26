## Technology & AI Use

**Can I use my laptop or phone during lecture?**
Laptops and tablets are fine for course-related purposes unless the instructor says otherwise for a specific activity. Phones should be put away and on silent. Full details: [Technology & AI Use Policy](../syllabus/#technology--ai-use-policy).

**Can I use technology during quizzes or exams?**
No. Quizzes and lab quizzes are closed-book and closed-technology. Exams are closed-book and closed-technology as well, with one exception — **one page of handwritten notes** is allowed. Printed slides are not permitted and will result in a zero on the exam. See [Technology & AI Use Policy](../syllabus/#technology--ai-use-policy).

**Can I use ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot, or similar tools for labs or homework?**
AI tools are **not allowed during lab sessions** at all, for any purpose. For homework, AI tools may be used only as a *tutor* (e.g., to explain a concept) — not to generate code on your behalf — and any use **must be disclosed**. Undisclosed AI use confirmed after the fact results in a zero for the entire assignment and may be reported as an academic integrity violation. Full policy: [Use of AI/LLM Tools](../syllabus/#use-of-aillm-tools).

**Is this a university-wide rule or just for this course?**
Both. GW's Office of the Provost prohibits submitting AI-generated content for evaluation by default, unless an instructor explicitly grants permission, per the [Generative AI Guidelines](https://provost.gwu.edu/guidelines-using-generative-artificial-intelligence-connection-academic-work-0) — this course's policy is a specific application of that rule and the [GW Code of Academic Integrity](https://students.gwu.edu/code-academic-integrity).

## Labs

**What language are labs in?**
Python.

**Do I need a GitHub account?**
Yes — you'll push all of your lab code to your own algorithms repository throughout the semester. Setup instructions are given in the first lab. Starter code and materials live at [github.com/Zirikly-teaching/algorithms-labs-gw26](https://github.com/Zirikly-teaching/algorithms-labs-gw26).

**Is lab attendance mandatory?**
Yes. TAs take attendance each session. If you can't make it, let us know as soon as possible along with the reason. See [Labs](../labs/#attendance).

**I'm stuck during lab — what should I do?**
Ask! TAs and lab instructors are there to help. Getting stuck is normal — reach out early rather than staying quiet.

**How many lab quizzes are there?**
Six, given during lab sessions, with your lowest score dropped. See [Labs](../labs/#assessments).

## Attendance & Participation

**Is lecture attendance mandatory?**
No, but it factors into your participation grade and is strongly recommended — frequent quizzes happen in lecture, and exams only allow handwritten notes, so attending and taking notes consistently is the best way to prepare. Lab attendance, unlike lecture, **is** mandatory. See [Participation & Attendance](../syllabus/#participation--attendance).

**What if I have to miss a lecture quiz for a legitimate reason?**
Notify the instructor as soon as possible (ideally before the missed session, if circumstances allow) and provide documentation. Approved absences are handled case-by-case and may include a makeup quiz, an alternate assignment, or excluding that quiz from your grade. See [Excused Absences](../syllabus/#excused-absences).
